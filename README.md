# Civic Hazard Reporter: AI for municipal hazard intake

Client: City of San José, Department of Transportation
Team: Shushruth Nandamuri, Nakieffe Stephens, Riya Murdeshwar, and Aditya Patro, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I led the project problem formulation and delivered the pitch narrative for our civic intake strategy.

## The problem
Residents new to San José struggle to identify which city department handles road and traffic hazards, causing dangerous conditions across 1,900 miles of streets to go unreported. Today, the intake process breaks because newcomers lack local institutional knowledge and abandon reporting altogether.

## What we built
* **Multilingual Intake & AI Processing:** Accepts voice or text reports from residents in their native language, automatically transcribing, translating, and categorizing hazard submissions for city staff.
* **Staff Review Dashboard & Public Feed:** Displays incoming reports side-by-side with AI-suggested priorities alongside a public community map showing real-time issue status.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Governance:** City staff manually review and approve every AI-generated summary and photo verification before official municipal work orders are generated.

## What I would do next
I would replace the shared staff access passcode with enterprise role-based authentication and integrate direct GIS mapping APIs to prevent location sync issues across duplicate reports.
